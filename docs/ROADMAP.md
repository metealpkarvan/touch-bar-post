# Keep the three jobs simple

Version 1.2 provides several saved notes, one-shot reminders and scrolling announcements through one message editor and list. New-message and update actions are explicit. The global 240–560-point strip-width preference, the selected message and valid edits persist. Existing v1 messages remain readable, and the downloadable app supports Intel and Apple Silicon.

The next priority is the physical Touch Bar and notification-delivery session in the device checklist. Check tap targets at the minimum and maximum strip widths with the normal Control Strip enabled, confirm that several saved notes remain easy to find after restarting, and observe deadline behaviour through sleep/wake and app quit.

Future work should address demonstrated usability problems or improve distribution signing and notarization. More dashboards, scenes, automatic rotation, accounts and AI services are outside this release's purpose. Preserve archive compatibility and recovery tests when making changes.
